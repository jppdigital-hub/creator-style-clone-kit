# Compact example: Nate Herk

ResourceStak: RS-002 Creator Style Clone
Version: 1.0.1

## Scope

This is a small example pulled from the Nate Herk research used for RS-001 Creator Business Clone. It shows how the style framework works. It isn't a complete study of Nate's content, and it doesn't claim to reveal his private production process.

## What showed up in the public sample

### OBSERVED

- The sample included pieces built around specific tools, workflows, and business outcomes.
- The public content connected videos with free resources, AI Automation Society, partner links, and other next steps.
- The public material included build-focused pieces and examples of work being put together in the open.

### INFERENCE

- The strongest packaging appears to promise a finished capability, such as building something useful, learning a tool quickly, or solving a business problem.
- Showing proof of the work early seems to help the viewer understand what they're about to get.
- The content often leaves something useful behind, such as a workflow, template, prompt, guide, or repository.

### REPORTED

- Any claim about Nate's private workflow, team, internal tools, or actual conversion results would need a direct source. This small example doesn't make those claims.

## Style DNA I could adapt

### Promise a useful result

**Job:** Give the viewer a reason to keep watching.
**Paul's version:** Start with the thing I'm trying to make, test, or figure out. Show the result when I have one.

### Let the work leave something behind

**Job:** Give the audience a useful next step after the video ends.
**Paul's version:** Turn a real framework, checklist, prompt, or working file into a resource when it's ready to share.

### Make the next move clear

**Job:** Keep the viewer from having to guess where to go.
**Paul's version:** Give one relevant next step, such as the resource, the longer explanation, or the project behind the work.

## Identity I would leave alone

- Nate's biography and career history.
- His personal stories, opinions, humor, and exact wording.
- His audience, community, products, partnerships, and professional programs.
- Any private team arrangement, software choice, economics, or analytics that isn't public.

## What this example doesn't establish

- It doesn't prove why a particular video performed well.
- It doesn't establish a complete visual-editing pattern.
- It doesn't reveal the internal production machine.
- It doesn't provide conversion rates or revenue figures.

Those are `UNKNOWN` until the evidence supports them.

## Source boundary

The source material is the public-facing Nate Herk case study packaged with [RS-001 Creator Business Clone](https://paullinehan.co/resources/creator-business-clone/). For a new study, build a fresh sample and label new observations separately.
